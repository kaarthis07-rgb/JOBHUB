📱 JOBHUB
Your All-In-One Personal Productivity & Career Companion
Purpose: JOBHUB is designed to be a completely private, offline personal workspace for students, job seekers, and developers to organize their daily lives and track their career goals in one place.
What you can do with it:
•
📅 Plan Your Day: Manage your daily schedule, interviews, or study routines with built-in alarms.
•
✅ Track Tasks: Keep on top of assignments, job applications, and daily to-dos with priority tags.
•
🚀 Manage Projects: Track your personal or portfolio projects, including the tech stack used and current status.
•
📝 Take Notes: Jot down quick ideas, interview prep notes, or learning summaries.
•
👤 Build Your Profile: Keep a personalized profile with your career goals, website/portfolio links, and occupation.
